# Hackathon workspace

Mode: Operate. Scope: login, dashboard, three blank feature routes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A usable hackathon shell whose empty areas are deliberately represented by skeletons.

OWN-WORLD: White surfaces, a soft lavender background, plum text, violet focus and action colors, Manrope interface typography, consistent thin Lucide icons, rounded bordered panels without decorative shadows.

STORY: Open the login page, enter the local demo, switch among dashboard and three feature pages, and sign out. Feature names and content remain placeholders.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Login pairs a lavender workspace illustration composed of skeleton geometry with a focused white login form. Dashboard uses a narrow persistent white sidebar, a compact top bar, and a spacious workspace with skeleton summary, chart, and list shapes. Mobile uses a compact header and four visible navigation tabs; this removes the need for an offscreen drawer and focus trapping. Active navigation uses a pale lavender fill. The only continuing motion is a reduced-motion-aware skeleton pulse.

FORM: Direct implementation of the user's pinned palette and narrow placeholder request. No speculative concept tournament, generated comp, or invented functionality. No seed key: direct shaping under new-work's narrow-request exemption. Code-first applies to this task only.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
